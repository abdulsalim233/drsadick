Biography

Surv. Dr. Abubakar Sadiq Mohammed is a dedicated Facilities and Real Estate Professional, Lecturer at Accra Technical University, and PhD Candidate in Land Management and Governance at KNUST.

With broad expertise spanning soft and hard facilities management, real estate valuation, and institutional asset operations, he actively contributes to developing smart, resilient infrastructure across educational, religious, and healthcare facilities.
